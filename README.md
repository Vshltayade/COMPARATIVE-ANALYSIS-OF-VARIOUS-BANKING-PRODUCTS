# Comparative Analysis of Various Banking Products

## 📖 Project Overview
This repository contains the MBA Finance project titled **“Comparative Analysis of Various Banking Products”**, submitted as part of the MBA (Finance) curriculum.  
The project focuses on analyzing and comparing various banking products with special reference to **State Bank of India (SBI)**, one of India’s largest public sector banks.

The study evaluates banking products, financial performance, and growth trends using standard financial analysis tools and secondary data sources.

---

## 🎯 Objectives of the Study
- To study various banking products offered by banks
- To analyze the financial growth of the selected bank
- To compare the performance of banking products
- To understand customer benefits provided by bank services
- To apply financial ratio analysis for performance evaluation

---

## 🏦 Scope of the Study
- Analysis of banking products such as:
  - Savings Accounts
  - Fixed & Recurring Deposits
  - Home Loans
  - Personal Loans
  - Education Loans
  - Investment Products
- Focused case study on **State Bank of India (SBI)**
- Based primarily on **secondary data** collected from:
  - Annual reports
  - Banking websites
  - Journals and articles

---

## 📊 Methodology Used
- **Research Type:** Descriptive Research  
- **Data Sources:**
  - Primary Data: Interviews and observations
  - Secondary Data: Annual reports, books, journals, websites
- **Analysis Tools:**
  - Ratio Analysis
  - Common Size Statements
  - Comparative Statements
  - Trend Analysis

---

## 📈 Financial Analysis Tools
The project uses the following financial ratios:

### 🔹 Profitability Ratios
- Gross Profit Ratio
- Net Profit Ratio
- Operating Profit Ratio
- Return on Capital Employed

### 🔹 Liquidity Ratios
- Current Ratio
- Quick Ratio

### 🔹 Solvency Ratios
- Debt-Equity Ratio
- Interest Coverage Ratio

### 🔹 Turnover Ratios
- Fixed Asset Turnover Ratio
- Inventory Turnover Ratio
- Receivables Turnover Ratio

### 🔹 Earnings Ratios
- Earnings Per Share (EPS)
- Price-Earnings Ratio (P/E)
- Return on Net Worth

---

## 🏛️ Company Profile
**State Bank of India (SBI)** is India’s largest public sector bank with a legacy of over 200 years.  
It offers a wide range of personal, corporate, rural, and international banking services and plays a significant role in India’s financial system.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations of the Study
- Dependence on secondary data
- Time and budget constraints
- Non-availability of confidential banking data
- Possibility of biased responses during interviews

---

## ✅ Conclusion
The study concludes that SBI maintains strong financial performance and offers a wide range of competitive banking products.  
Financial ratio analysis indicates that both public and private sector banks play a crucial role in economic development, with SBI holding a dominant position in India’s banking sector.
